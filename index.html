From our region

Breton ingredients, baked in Nantes

Breton buckwheat

The flour of our famous galettes gives the cup its toasted flavour and its crunch.

Breton seaweed

Harvested on the Brittany coast, it makes the cup stronger and keeps our straws firm until the last sip.

A short supply chain

Our ingredients come from our own region, not from across the oceans. Less transport, more local jobs.

Innovation

What makes our cup different

Crunch-Lock coating

A thin natural plant layer inside the cup keeps it crisp for four hours, even with a hot drink.

Edible straws

Made from seaweed and rice. They stay firm in your drink instead of going soggy like paper.

Questions

Good to know

Does the cup leak or go soft?

No. The Crunch-Lock coating keeps the cup sealed and crispy for four hours, with hot or cold drinks.

What if I don’t want to eat it?

That’s fine. The cup is made only of food ingredients, so it can simply go in the compost.

Is it gluten-free?

Buckwheat is naturally gluten-free. As a student project we have not certified it yet, so people with coeliac disease should wait for the official label.

Where can I find it?

Our goal for year one is 200 partner coffee shops in France, then Europe, Japan, South Korea and the USA.
